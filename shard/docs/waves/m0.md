(`rig/scenarios/m1*`, `m2*`, `M2Map.java`, `M2OracleTest`, `evidence/m1/`, `m2/`) or claiming a milestone
gate. `evidence/` is the run evidence, archived outside this repository. Rows: W2.22 (M0), W5.6 (M1, M2). Their owned paths and WPs are in [`history.md`](history.md). Index, ownership rules and live
contracts: [`WAVES.md`](../../WAVES.md). Paths are relative to the repository root.

M0 (`ARCHITECTURE.md` §10): two stock clients join a real bundled map and one reconnects; a player builds a
conveyor line that a processor controls; a power consumer runs; a turret shoots a wave unit; L1–L3 green, L5 on boat.

**M0 passed (W2.22, 2026-10-29).**
- L1: `M0OracleTest` on fortress is exact against vanilla for 18,492 frames. A player's queue builds unloader,
  conveyors, router and three duos, a processor switches a conveyor, solar panels power a lancer, and the duos kill
  wave 1. `M0RigMapOracleTest` is exact on the rig's map for 13,563 frames.
- L5: `rig/scenarios/m0.rig` passes 78/78 on Shard and on vanilla, and `shard-two-clients.rig` 48/48. Evidence is
  in `evidence/m0/`.
- Nine one-line breaks each fail the fixture.
- Fixed on the way: a player's new unit drifted by its spawn impulse (`RemoteSync.snap`), and construct sites of

  sends the server's own pose); `m0.rig` didn't fail on server WARN lines. Fixed by W4.8: its last `assert log server !/.../`
  fails on every WARN but the two a busy VM makes ("stalled for N ms", "N ticks behind"). Checked against the committed
  evidence log, not re-run.

# M1, every default map on a stock client

M1 (`ARCHITECTURE.md` §10): a vanilla client joins all 18 default maps, walks and sees cores, with no desync log lines.

**M1 passed (W5.6, 2026-11-01): 18 of 18 maps clean.**
- L5: `rig/scenarios/m1.rig`, 1 client, Shard hosting the 18 maps with `--join round-robin`, so alpha's 18 joins land
  on them in vanilla's order. On each: the world has the map's size, the client sees as many cores of its team as the
  map has (passage 2), each a core building at its tile, and walks at least 2 tiles. An `exec` step then records the
  map in `m1-maps.tsv`, "clean" or "FAIL" with the client's desync or error lines and the server's ERROR or WARN lines
  since the map before, so a failure names its map and every map is still visited. The end checks are `m0.rig`'s:
  a clean client log, no server Exception or ERROR after `ready`, no WARN but the two load lines.
  260/260 steps; evidence in `evidence/m1/`.
- The script is generated (18 identical blocks); edit all of them alike.
- Two clients (W6.5): `rig/scenarios/m1-two-clients.rig`, generated by `m1-two-clients.py`, keeps the round robin: bravo's
  first join takes maze, then each map takes one join, alpha and bravo in turn, and the other client follows with
  `/world`. On each map both clients check the map's size and cores, each other's position, both blocks built from
  their own build plans (a conveyor and a copper wall; a duct and a beryllium wall on Erekir's domain) and each other's
  unit walking, and `m1-maps.tsv` records the map. 641/641 steps, 18 of 18 maps clean; evidence in
  `evidence/m1-two-clients/`.

# M2, build, mine, convey, power and defend on Serpulo

M2 (`ARCHITECTURE.md` §10): build, mine, convey, power and defend work on Serpulo maps.

**M2 passed (W5.6, 2026-11-01).**
- L1: `M2OracleTest` on fortress, through `M0Run`: a player's queue builds two mechanical drills on copper, a conveyor
  line into the core, two drills on coal feeding four combustion generators, a battery, a lancer and a power node that
  joins them. The player leaves at frame 2,939 (`M0Run.leave`: vanilla's `onDisconnect`, Shard's `HostInputs.Leave`);
  vanilla's save loads into both at frame 3,000 (`M0Run.saveAndLoad`, as `SupportRun`'s); wave 1's two daggers die to
  the lancer at frame 18,416. Exact for 18,416 frames, 15,416 after the load, no tolerance; save chunks equal at the
  end. Its checks: copper reached the core (781 -> 792) and kept coming after the load (810 -> 1000, two drills' rate),
  the generators burned, the battery charged, the core unit despawned on leaving, the lancer fired 5 times.
  `theSameBaseOnTheRigsMap` plays it on `m2.msav` with the same numbers.
- L5: `rig/scenarios/m2.rig`, 2 clients, the same script on Shard and on vanilla, both 77/77: alpha builds the copper
  side, bravo the coal and power side; both see each other's blocks, the graph, the charge and the copper; bravo
  rejoins; `runwave`, and the lancer kills wave 1. Evidence in `evidence/m2/`.
- `m2.msav` (`M2Map.java`) exists because hosting fortress runs its generation filters with a new seed, so each server
  puts the ores elsewhere; it freezes the ores the oracle's hosting made and empties `genfilters`.
- Rig: a 2x2 block clicked at a tile's centre can land a tile off (`InputHandler.tileX` rounds the pointer less half a
  tile, and the centre is the rounding edge). Place 2x2 blocks at world (8x+3, 8y+3) and assert their origin tiles.
- Open: no save on the rig (Shard's console has no save and load command); the oracle covers the save. No breaks were
  written for these gates: they compose families whose own fixtures carry breaks.
